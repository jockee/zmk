Everyday shortcuts

ActionKeys
Go to a desktopHold K + right home row (6–9), or hold R + left home row (1–5)
Back to the last desktopHold K + tap Y
Move window to a desktopHold W+K together + right home row, or hold R+, together + left home row
Next app on this desktopHold E + tap Tab. Also hold A to go back
Next window, same appHold E + tap Esc. Also hold A to go back
Screenshot area to clipboardScreenshot key, left bottom row, 2nd from the outer edge
RaycastHold E + tap Space
Type a symbolTap SYM, then the symbol key. SYM stays armed for 2 seconds
Media and brightnessTap SYM, then hold O and press the key
Cmd + numberHold W + right home row (6–0), or hold , + left home row (1–5)
Flash new firmwareRun ./flash.sh in ~/web/zmk, then hold Magic + tap Esc

Big label = tap. Small label = what the key does when held.

SYM layer: symbols

Tap SYM (right home row, inner key), then one of these.

NUM layer: digits, math and media

Tap SYM, then tap O (right thumb): NUM stays on until you tap O again, or press Esc or Enter (they do their thing and leave NUM).
